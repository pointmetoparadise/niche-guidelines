Point Me to Paradise Travel — Internal Program

Niche Page Manager Program

A selective opportunity for approved PMTP agents to manage and grow company-owned niche brands focused on specific destinations and travel styles.

This document outlines the structure, expectations, and policies of the PMTP Niche Page Manager Program. Read it in full before applying. Participation is a privilege, not an entitlement — and understanding these guidelines is the first step.

01
Program Overview
What This Is

Point Me to Paradise Travel is building a portfolio of PMTP-owned niche pages focused on specific destinations, travel styles, and specialty products. Approved agents may apply for the opportunity to manage one of these niche pages as the designated specialist/specialists under the PMTP brand.

The goal is to build true specialists under the PMTP umbrella — creating stronger consumer trust, improving conversion on niche leads, and giving agents the opportunity to grow a recognizable specialty within the company.

The model in plain terms: The page belongs to PMTP. The niche manager/managers operate it. The leads generated from that page go to the assigned agent/agents under standard PMTP commission structure. Clients booked remain the agent's clients.

Two Types of Niches

Destination Niches
  • Italy, Greece, Mexico, Caribbean, etc.
  • Focus: geography, routing, seasons, hotels, logistics, ideal client fit
  • You must be willing to invest into researching, knowing, and understanding the destination deeply
Product / Style Niches
  • Cruising, Disney, Sandals, Honeymoons, etc.
  • Focus: brands, booking systems, experience design, selling points, qualification questions
  • You must be willing to invest into researching, knowing, and understanding the niched area of specialty scope deeply

These are different animals and the expectations for each manager will reflect which type of niche they hold.

02
Ownership & Branding
Non-Negotiable

This is the most important section in this document. Please read it carefully.

All "Point Me to…" niche pages, branding, websites, social handles, lead funnels, and PMTP-generated assets are and remain the sole property of Point Me to Paradise Travel — period.

What This Means for Agents

  • Agents do not own the PMTP niche name, page, logo, website, content library, or lead funnel
  • Agents do not acquire any rights to a niche page by managing it over time
  • If an agent leaves PMTP for any reason, the niche page and all associated assets remain with PMTP
  • Clients personally acquired and booked by the agent through a niche page remain the property of the agent upon exit from the PMTP partnership — the client relationship belongs to the agent, not the page
  • PMTP may have more than one contributing expert in a niche, even if one person is the lead manager
  • No separate brand ownership is granted or implied by niche assignment

Being assigned a niche is a professional opportunity extended by PMTP. It is not a business partnership, ownership stake, or permanent assignment. It is a role — with real benefits — that requires meeting real standards.

03
Eligibility & Approval
How Assignment Works

Niche page assignments are not automatic and are not based solely on interest. They are awarded by application and approval based on expertise, availability, niche fit, professionalism, responsiveness, and PMTP's business needs.

"I love Italy" is not a qualification. "I've booked 14 Italy itineraries, completed destination training, and can respond to leads within two hours" is.

Evaluation Criteria

Applications are reviewed on a combination of the following factors:

  • Demonstrated knowledge of the niche destination or product
  • Real booking experience in that niche
  • Relevant training, certifications, or supplier relationships
  • Response time and day-to-day availability
  • Ability to contribute content (written, video, social) for the page
  • Willingness to accept and properly service PMTP-generated leads
  • Sales ability and demonstrated conversion track record
  • Professionalism, follow-through, and communication standards
  • Good standing with PMTP and the leadership team
  • Long-term commitment to building that specialty

Good Standing Requirement

Agents must be in good standing with PMTP at the time of application and throughout their assignment. Niche management is an extended responsibility — it is not available to agents with unresolved performance issues.

04
Manager Expectations
What's Expected of You

Niche managers are expected to actively represent and help grow their specialty. Managing a niche page is not passive. It requires consistent investment of time, knowledge, and professional presence.

Core Responsibilities

  • Respond quickly to niche leads — timeliness is non-negotiable
  • Stay actively current on that destination or product
  • Accept and properly service all PMTP-generated leads in that specialty — if you cannot manage a lead due to lack of time or capacity, you agree to refer that lead to another capable agent on the PMTP team rather than allow it to go unserviced
  • Represent PMTP professionally across all client touchpoints
  • Help create or supply niche-specific content to support the page
  • Maintain the page's relevance and accuracy over time
  • Communicate proactively with PMTP leadership if availability changes

Ongoing Accountability

  • Monthly content contribution (written tips, reels, social posts, or similar)
  • Participation in periodic page reviews with PMTP leadership
  • Adherence to lead follow-up standards set by PMTP
  • Minimum booking or engagement activity over time (reviewed periodically)
  • Willingness to work within a team model if niche volume grows

If you reach a point where you can no longer meet these expectations, the right move is to communicate that to PMTP leadership early — not to let leads go cold and the page go stale.

05
Leads, Commission & Client Ownership
How the Money Works

Lead Assignment

Leads generated directly through PMTP-owned niche pages are assigned to the niche manager/managers unless otherwise determined by PMTP leadership.

All leads follow the standard PMTP commission structure. There is no separate or elevated commission rate for niche page leads.

Client Ownership

Clients booked by the agent through niche page leads remain the working agent's clients in accordance with PMTP IC agreement and house policies — provided the agent remains in good standing and continues meeting PMTP standards.

Lead Reassignment

If a niche manager/managers are unavailable, unresponsive, or unable to properly service incoming leads, PMTP may temporarily or permanently reassign those leads or the page itself. Leads are an opportunity — not a guarantee.

  • Slow or absent response to leads may result in temporary reassignment
  • Consistent failure to service leads may result in loss of niche assignment
  • Agents who leave PMTP forfeit all niche page lead rights upon departure — however, any clients already personally attained and booked through the page prior to departure may follow the agent, as those client relationships remain the agent's own
06
PMTP Reserved Rights
Company Authority

Point Me to Paradise Travel reserves the right to take any of the following actions at its discretion, with or without prior notice:

  • Reassign a niche page to a different agent/agents
  • Place a niche page on temporary or permanent pause
  • Require a performance improvement plan before continuation
  • Remove management rights if standards are not being met
  • Expand a niche into a team model if volume grows
  • Add contributing agents to any niche, even with an existing lead manager
  • Retire or merge a niche page based on business needs
  • Review and adjust program structure at any time

These rights exist to protect the integrity of the program and the quality of the PMTP brand. A niche assignment is not a franchise. It is a role within a company-owned system.

How to Apply
The Process

If you've read this document in full and believe you're a strong candidate for a niche page, the next step is completing the PMTP Niche Page Manager Application.

Before You Apply, Ask Yourself

  • Do I have real booking experience in this niche — not just interest?
  • Can I respond to leads within a few hours during normal business hours?
  • Am I willing to contribute content to this page consistently?
  • Am I prepared to represent PMTP professionally in this specialty?
  • Do I understand that this page belongs to PMTP — not to me?
  • If lead volume grows, am I open to working within a team?

If you answered yes to all of the above — we want to hear from you. Submit your application and PMTP leadership will review and follow up.
